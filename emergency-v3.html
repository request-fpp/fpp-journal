H2: First Thing in a Plumbing Emergency: Shut the Water Off

Close the water first, then call. On most of these calls the pipe repair is the small part. What costs the money is the wet drywall and the floor, and that part grows every minute the water runs.

If one fixture is leaking, close the small stop valve at that fixture. Under a sink there are two on the wall, hot and cold. A toilet has one behind it, low, near the floor. Turn it to the right until it stops. If it's the water heater, close the valve on the cold line at the top of the tank. If you can't tell where the water is coming from, or the small valve does nothing, close the main shut-off valve for the whole house. The main shut-off valve guide has photos of where to look for that valve in a house here, and it shows how to close it.

Two things go wrong at this step. The small stops in a lot of houses here are builder grade, and one that hasn't been turned in ten years may not close at all, or it starts to leak the moment you touch it. Our guide to a leaking shut-off valve under the sink explains why. And a valve that's stuck should not be forced with a wrench: if it snaps, you have two leaks. Tell us on the phone that it won't turn, and we'll walk you to the next valve. If you ask, we text you the main valve guide while we talk. That doesn't work for everybody. A sewer backup is the other kind of emergency, and the rule there is different. Nothing is spraying, so there is nothing to close. Stop using water in the whole house, the washer and the dishwasher too, because every gallon you send down comes back up at the lowest drain.

H2: Burst Pipes After a Hard Freeze: Where They Break

Nobody can tell you ahead of time which pipe a hard freeze will take. But pipe breaks in a freeze come from the same few places.

In a garage it's the supply line in the wall that feeds the outside spigot, when that wall has no insulation. In the attic it's any pipe that runs bare through thin insulation, and a burst pipe up there floods the whole house under it. In older houses it's the pipes in outside walls, where the insulation has thinned out over the years. And after a hard freeze we get more calls for leaks under the slab. The ground under a house should be warmer than the air, and we can't tell you why it happens. It does, and that work is slab leak repair.

H2: Before It's an Emergency: Find the Main Shut-Off Valve

Every leak on this page gets smaller when somebody in the house can close the water in the first minute. Two more things do a lot of the work. Have the water pressure checked once a year. Over 80 PSI the whole system is under stress, and the weak part gives first, a cartridge or a supply line.

The other one is an automatic water shut-off valve. It sits on the main line and watches the flow. When a pipe bursts and water moves the way it never does in normal use, the valve sends a message to your phone, and depending on how it's set up, it closes the water by itself.
