Water on the floor

Close the main valve. In most homes here it is in a box at the curb or where the water line enters the house, and our shut-off guide shows both with photos. That stops the flow into the ground and stops it coming up through the floor. Then call. Our emergency plumbing line is answered by a person at any hour, and a slab leak that has surfaced goes to the front of the schedule.

No hot water

Look at the water heater first. If there is water on the floor around the base, close the cold valve on top of the tank and call, that tank is done. If the floor is dry: on a gas heater check whether the pilot is lit, on an electric one check the breaker. If it comes back and quits again within a day, do not keep resetting it, call. [Water heater repair and replacement →]

Sewer backing up

Stop putting water down every drain in the house: no flushing, no showers, no washer, no dishwasher. Do not plunge the lowest drain, you will push it into the tub. Close the toilet supply valve if it is overflowing. Then call, a sewer backup goes to the front of the schedule. [Sewer line repair and camera inspection →]

No water pressure

Ask a neighbor whether they have water. If the whole street is dry, it is the city. If it is only your house, check that the main valve at the house is fully open and look at the meter box for standing water. A PRV that failed drops pressure in the whole house at once. Do not open the PRV yourself, call.
